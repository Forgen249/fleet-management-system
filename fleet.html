About the Fleet Management System

The Fleet Management System provides a centralized platform for managing departmental vehicles and related fleet activities.

The system can support vehicle registration, allocation, maintenance tracking, fuel management, driver information, inspections and reporting.

  • Centralized vehicle records
  • Vehicle allocation and tracking
  • Maintenance management
  • Fuel and mileage monitoring
  • Reports and operational analytics

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about fleet management

What is the Fleet Management System?

It is a centralized system designed to manage departmental vehicles, drivers, maintenance, allocation, fuel records and fleet reports.

How do I register a vehicle?

Authorized users can access the vehicle management module and enter the required vehicle information before submitting the record for registration.

How can I request a vehicle?

A user can submit a vehicle request through the system. The request can then be reviewed and approved by the relevant authorized officer.

Can the system track vehicle maintenance?

Yes. The maintenance module can be used to record servicing, repairs, maintenance dates, costs and other relevant information.

Can the system generate reports?

Yes. Fleet information can be organized into reports to support monitoring, accountability, planning and management decisions.

Who can access the system?

Access should be restricted to authorized users according to their assigned roles and permissions.
